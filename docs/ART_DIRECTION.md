# Art direction — Premium Stylized 3D
Visual target: original cozy toy-like miniature kitchen, rounded chunky forms, warm inviting palette, appetizing layered food, soft shadows, expressive animation. No copying Overcooked assets or branding. Reference images discussed in chat are inspiration only and not licensed runtime files.
Palette baseline: cream #FFF0D5, warm wood #C88B52, teal #358E89, tomato #E84C43, lettuce #65B94D, cheese #F7C54B, charcoal #28343A. Warm key light, cool fill, restrained ambient occlusion; avoid heavy postprocessing. Materials stylized PBR-lite, controlled roughness, minimal noisy textures. Isometric-like orthographic 3/4 camera, proposal yaw 45°, elevation 55°, zoom fit kitchen with HUD safe areas. Roof omitted, walls cut away where obstructing. Floors delineate walkable routes. Readability before detail.
## Character bible
Shared humanoid rig, rounded heads about 40% of height, mitten-like hands, short limbs, clear face. Chef A coral apron and tall hat, Chef B teal apron and shorter cap; distinct silhouette + active ring (not color alone). Customers share compatible rigs with modular hair, clothing, accessories; VIP and critic later. Staff roles identified by prop and silhouette. No separate skeleton for each skin. Carry socket aligned with plate and food, no intersections at camera distance.
## Food and props
Hero assets: chef, burger, stove, serving counter. Bun glossy soft golden; patty dark sear marks; lettuce curled bright edges; tomato visible seeds; cheese soft folds. Raw/prepped/cooked/overcooked/burnt states distinct at camera scale. Modular counters, walls, floor, bins, tables, chairs. Consistent grid footprint, pivot at floor/base, meters, named sockets for food and hands. No final default cubes, missing texture, floating items or mixed art styles.
## Animation
Required slice: idle, walk, run, carry walk/run, pickup, place, chop loop, fry interaction, wash loop, serve, success, panic. Later slip, extinguisher, oven, stirring. Fast anticipation/contact/recovery; animation must never delay logical interaction unnecessarily. Shared rig, reusable clips, crossfade locomotion, hand sockets. Food pop on assembly, steam and subtle bounce; fans and signage ambient motion. Test repeated actions, interruption and clipping.
## UI and visual feedback
Top order cards with food icons, modifiers and patience; timer/objective top center; chef/action prompt lower left; cash lower right; warnings near station plus global cue. Never obscure walking routes. Management separate panel with build grid, cost, comparison, confirm/cancel and undo. Results show revenue/costs/orders. Responsive 1280x720 and 1920x1080, keyboard focus, text + icon warnings, reduced motion option. Use original typography or licensed font. Avoid tiny text and hover-only controls.
## VFX
Steam, sizzling droplets, chop crumbs, wash bubbles, burn smoke, serving sparkle, dash streak. Pooled particles; opacity and size limited to avoid occlusion. Warning icon before burn; fire later. Success pop and sound synchronized. Reduced motion disables shake and excess particles.
## Asset pipeline
Review included original AI concept board and crops; approve hero chef + Burger Town target at G0, create or procure licensed GLB models, rig and animate, export with clean transforms, optimize KTX2/mesh compression as supported, validate in engine and record license and sizes. Blender procedural modeling is allowed; no claim that generated illustrations are meshes. Review screenshot at gameplay camera before approving. Keep source files separate from runtime. Proposed budgets in TECH_DESIGN.md. Missing assets listed in manifest; source/rights must be documented before import. No paid asset purchases without Producer approval.
