# Game design document
## Vision
Premium stylized 3D, angled orthographic camera, original characters and brand. Desktop web first; single-player with switchable chefs and deterministic staff AI. Hybrid world: persistent restaurants plus challenges. Target casual/strategy players; sessions 5–10 minutes; long-term career. Approximate 70% arcade / 30% management. No online co-op in initial release.
## Core loop
Prepare menu, staff and equipment → open for a timed shift → fetch, prep, cook, assemble, serve, clean → settle revenue and reputation → buy equipment / train staff / rearrange kitchen → next day. Planning has no timer. Pause freezes all simulation. Shift target 300 seconds; tutorial introduces actions sequentially without timer. Orders stop spawning near end; pending orders expire at closing. Successful shift meets service target; failure lowers reputation but never deletes career. Recovery shift uses basic ingredients and no wage charge if insolvent (proposed).
## Controls and interactions
WASD move, E pick/place, Space operate station, Shift dash, Q switch chef, Tab expand order list, Esc pause. Camera fixed angled view; active chef ring and station highlight. Interaction selects nearest valid station in facing direction with deterministic tie-break; show prompt before action. One carried item/container per chef. Collision prevents walking through counters; dash cannot pass walls. No physics-driven random food loss. Space held to chop/wash, release preserves progress; cooking continues unattended. Invalid action gives short sound and text, no item deletion. Trash requires deliberate interaction. Keyboard focus loss pauses and clears held input.
## Burger Town vertical slice
One restaurant, 2 switchable chefs (inactive chef optionally assigned prep or wash tasks), 5–8 customer visual variants, three recipes, persistent 7-day playable career with repeatable days, equipment purchasing and layout editing. Customer seating is visible; serving counter matches orders automatically by exact recipe, oldest valid order first. Wrong dish remains on counter, no credit. Serving clears ticket, grants money and tip; dirty plate returns to wash station after customer leaves. Customers queue if seats full, then leave when patience expires. Staff cannot duplicate items or reserve occupied stations indefinitely; unreachable tasks cancel and display warning. Role automation must not solve all cooking.
### Recipes (initial tuning)
| Recipe | Required ingredients | Price |
|---|---|---:|
| Classic | bun + cooked patty | 12 |
| Cheese | bun + cooked patty + cheese | 15 |
| Garden | bun + cooked patty + chopped lettuce + sliced tomato | 17 |
Bun and cheese ready-to-use; patty raw→cooking→perfect→overcooked→burnt; lettuce/tomato raw→prepped. Chopping 2s; patty perfect at 8–12s, overcooked after 12s, burnt after 16s. Overcooked accepted at reduced tip; raw/burnt rejected. Assembly on clean plate, order-independent ingredients, no duplicates. Wash 3s. Burnt item can be trashed; fire event later, not required for prototype. Timer cues with icon, color and sound. Ingredients consumed only on fetch; no duplicated entities. Starting supply replenished each day; ingredient cost recorded per fetch, including waste. 
### Initial balancing (proposed)
Start cash 100; two chefs, one prep board, one stove, sink, serving counter, ingredient bins, assembly counter, four plates, two customer tables. Orders every 18–25s seeded; patience 60s; day 1 target 6 successful orders, day 2 8, day 3+ 10; scale only after playtest. Tips 0–4 based on patience/quality. Daily staff cost 15 after tutorial, overhead 5, per ingredient cost 1; revenue = sales + tips; profit = revenue − ingredient cost − wages − overhead. No real-money purchases. Upgrades: second board 35, second stove 60, plate rack +2 plates 25. Layout uses grid, valid reachable routes and undo; purchase only commits after valid placement. Money cannot go below zero on purchase. Shift results show costs, orders, misses, waste, reputation; save after settlement and management transactions, not during shift.
## Progression and world
City hub is interactive miniature map, not free-roaming. Each persistent restaurant has up to five visual tiers: stall, small shop, restaurant, premium, flagship. Unlocks via reputation, milestones and cash rather than grind alone. Long-term worlds: Burger Town (burgers/fries/hot dogs), Little Italy (pizza/pasta), Tokyo Street (sushi/ramen), Asian Night Market (pho/banh mi), Tropical Paradise (seafood/BBQ), Grand Metropolitan (steak/desserts). Each adds a new gameplay verb or constraint, not just faster timers. Older restaurants can be delegated to managers and earn capped simulated income on in-game days only. No offline accrual. Player mastery, reputation, staff training and skill tree (cooking/management/business) are later expansions. Automation via conveyors/processors is limited by cost, space and throughput; special orders preserve manual play.
## Modes and scope
Career is must-have; Challenge (fixed objective, replayable), Endless (increasing pressure, loss after missed-order threshold), Sandbox (isolated save, unlimited funds) are post-G2 candidates. Co-op deferred, separate architecture approval. Long-term aspiration six worlds, 60+ dishes, 40+ equipment, 50+ challenges, 15–25h career is not a delivery commitment. MVP after G2: Burger Town plus stable career, challenge and endless if approved; remaining worlds gated. No feature can bypass polished slice approval.
## Fun test
Representative minute: fetch patty, start cooking, switch chef to chop lettuce, return before burn, assemble and serve while next order arrives; meaningful tradeoff between time, quality and route. Observe first-time players completing order without coaching, understanding warnings, recovering from mistakes, wanting another day. Producer decides fun; no automated pass substitutes.
